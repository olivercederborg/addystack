# Slim scope

**Apply when** you're shaping a plan, a stack, a review round, or a `Next` list, or
deferring a change.

Its index line states the rule. A follow-up that waits gets no separate or stacked PR
unless asked. An approver's ask is built now because they re-approve it quickly, and a new PR needs a
review of its own.

**Why:** "its getting way too out of hand. lets keep it simple" · "but isnt it the best
long term solution ?".

**Pattern:**
- **PRs**: vertical slices of up to about 1000 changed lines.
- **Review loops**: stop at the skill's round cap and list what's still open.
- **Option `a`**: inside the PR's scope, or it names the scope change it makes.

**Tell:** a plan that grew without the user asking.
