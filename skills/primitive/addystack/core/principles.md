# Principles

How {{user}} wants work done. Before applying a principle, read its file in full from
`{{kit}}/principles/`.{{taste}}

- **[do-it-before-asking](do-it-before-asking.md)**: about to ask {{user}}, hand them a step, or answer from code what live data shows. Look it up, probe it, or drive it first.
- **[fix-the-cause-in-scope](fix-the-cause-in-scope.md)**: fixing a bug or finding. Fix the cause and its siblings; out of scope, it's a follow-up unless {{user}} asked.
- **[reuse-before-adding](reuse-before-adding.md)**: adding a service, type, file, or dependency. Name what you checked first.
- **[check-exposure](check-exposure.md)**: weighing compatibility, rollout, or severity. Find who can hit it today.
- **[prove-it-works](prove-it-works.md)**: about to say done, fixed, or safe, or to rest a decision on a claim. Check the real artifact or source.
- **[explain-the-number](explain-the-number.md)**: about to trust, report, or act on a measured number. Name what limits it and what else it could be measuring.
- **[attack-the-premise](attack-the-premise.md)**: two fixes failed the same check. Test their shared assumption.
- **[encode-lessons-in-structure](encode-lessons-in-structure.md)**: a correction repeats, or a rule is being added. Enforce it, and delete the prose.
- **[slim-scope](slim-scope.md)**: shaping a plan, stack, review round, or `Next` list. Recommend the smallest option.
